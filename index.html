How does your pricing and service compare to other companies?

We're owner operated, and our high temperature, high flow rinsing system is built to remove all previous residue. Our prices are all-inclusive, with no add-ons, up-selling or surcharges.

Do you do business over the phone?

We encourage customers to use the online instant quote form on this website. We also accept emails. Due to high demand, phone calls may go unanswered.

What if I overestimate the room sizes?

No need to worry. We will make the necessary adjustments. Filling out the form as accurately as possible helps us book the right amount of time.

How long does it take to dry?

On average the carpets will be dry to the touch in 3 to 5 hours. Furniture with wooden feet should wait until the next morning, and move-in cleanings should be booked at least one day before the move.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please don't cancel! If you must, give us as much notice as possible. Last-minute cancellations leave a time slot we can't fill.

What are your payment terms?

We accept cheques, Visa, MasterCard, Amex and e-Transfer. Payment is due at the time of cleaning.
